# SEVEN DAYS — Revised Story Outline (DRAFT FOR REVIEW)

Status: draft only. No game files have been changed. Please review and mark each open question before implementation.

## Decision: the two chapters stay separate for now

The creator has decided to merge the Day Five and Day Six chapters into a single Day Five. Every beat from both days is kept, in the original order, inside that one chapter:

1. Original Day Five beats (painted street, Grinwick, Mr. Hush, ink factory, Inkwright, three-way pursuit, editing room, film tear).
2. Original Day Six beats (Miss Merrily, Buttonbell, wrong-answer doors, neighborhood, Keepsake facility, broadcast control room, the Splicemaster's explanation, final channel).
3. Proposed additions (Bodies in the Crawlspace, the ritual, the stones) are inserted at the point each one fits, not as replacements.

The combined chapter is titled "Day Five" in the menu. The old "Day Six" menu entry is removed as a separate chapter, but its content plays inside Day Five after the film tear.

Open: whether the combined chapter keeps a single title card ("DAY FIVE") or shows a second card when the broadcast begins ("DAY SIX — THE BROADCAST") as a mid-chapter break.

## Hard rule from the creator

**Nothing existing is removed.** Every scene, line and beat already in the screenplay and in the episode files stays. The notes below are ADDITIONS that extend the game. Where an addition would overlap an existing beat, it is inserted before, between or after that beat rather than replacing it. The game should be longer, darker and more immersive, with real pacing and meat in every episode, not a shorter version.

Where this document previously said "replaces" or "removes", read it as "adds a new level or sequence alongside; the original stays."

## Summary of notes from the creator

- Day Five is no longer the painted cartoon studio. It becomes **"Bodies in the Crawlspace"**, a claustrophobic crawlspace level.
- The night-99 forest sequence becomes **one long night**, not a series of resets.
- The Day Five ending is a ritual sequence (stones, a fire, a holy cleanse) that bleeds into Day Six.
- Day Seven becomes a **hospital coma** reveal, followed by a restart at the island campsite.

## Proposed structure

### Prologue — The Island (plane crash)
- The protagonist is on a plane with their sports team when it crashes on an island.
- The protagonist is the only survivor and must run through the burning wreckage.
- The VHS tape in the plane, and the memories it triggers, open the way back.

### Day One — Hollowmere Galleria
Keep the existing script as written. The search of the Galleria, Keeper Thatch, the flood, the foundations run and being captured are unchanged; the day's new additions are the discoveries (photographs, the SEVEN DAYS mark, the cassette recorder) exactly as scripted.

### Day Two — The Nostalgia Warehouse
- Opens in a real burning area that cools into the Warehouse.
- The protagonist pushes a shopping trolley that represents the **Morrow Moppet**.
- The Fog and collapsing aisles remain, and the day still ends in the Fire Room.
- Placing the four stones correctly frees the rest of the path.

### Day Three — Render Distance
- Opens with a forest: an upside-down waterfall, floating islands, clouds upside down.
- The protagonist feels a wrong familiarity with the environment, as if they have come home, even though they have been lost.
- A siren begins, getting stranger with each return. Each return adds reports of the accident, death certificates and missing-person flyers.
- A small lost child asks for help; the protagonist follows them towards safety.
- The forest becomes a burning ritual site by late in the day, with a pentagram and forgotten souls.
- The Cleaverback's return, the debugging chamber, the Velocity Wraith's warnings and the 404 door remain unchanged.
- Placing the four stones correctly frees the rest of the path.
- Reaching the campsite leads into the forest. The Burned Accomplice appears to multiply: four of them. A spider-like creature crawls through the canopy.
- The chase ends at a giant hole under the largest tree, which leads down into the next level, **RUN RABBIT RUN**.
- An older witch figure in white gives the ritual: place four stones (north, south, east, west). If the player fails or the fire goes out, it is a game over.

### Day Four — 99 Nights / 404 Forest (revised to one long night)
- Replaces the repeated-night sequence with one continuous night in the forest.
- Milestone beats (river, cabin furniture, footprints, breath count, silence, recordings in branches) can be kept as single discoveries in the same night, if desired.
- Ends with the Pale Thread and the root tunnel, as before.

### Day Five — "Bodies in the Crawlspace" (NEW, replaces the painted studio)
- Setting: a claustrophobic crawlspace under a house, with bodies and a man who talks to them.
- The man is described as a psychopath; he speaks to the dead as if they were friends.
- The chaser is the **Burned Accomplice**: a small crawling creature with burned-off skin, a round head, burned-off ears, a giant smile with sharp teeth, and eyes. It moves like a spider and chases the player the whole time.
- The crawlspace is tight; the player is captured and taken to a hatch-room "interlude."
- In the interlude, dead "homies" hang around. The player is seated before a television.
- The TV shows the prologue tape: the plane crash on the island.

### Day Five / Day Six handoff — the Ritual
- Four stones must be placed correctly (north, south, east, west). The last stone (south) sits in the basement of "Big Brother's" house.
- Placing the south stone locks the player in the room. A radio gives the instructions:
- Say goodbye to friends.
- Set yourself on fire.
- A holy cleanse in the toilet, which has been prayed over ("Big Brother's" toilet).
- The creature inside can only be freed by suffering the pain of the burning.
- The player passes out.

### Day Seven — Hospital Reveal (REVISED ending)
- The protagonist wakes in a hospital bed. They have been in a coma for seven days, and the doctors were about to turn off life support.
- On leaving, the protagonist hears the siren again, then it fades.
- The protagonist arrives at the first campsite beside the plane wreckage.
- It starts over.
