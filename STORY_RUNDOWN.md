# SEVEN DAYS — Story Rundown (plain language)

This is the whole game in plain words: what happens, in what order, and which characters appear. It includes the additions from the creator's new notes, each one marked **[NEW]** so it is clear what has been added and what was already there.

## Format — read this first
- **In the fiction, the player only watches.** The main character does everything by themselves. The player never controls the character.
- **Occasionally following the player:** the character is played in first person and the player moves with them, still without choosing what happens (for example, the chase and capture beats).
- Nothing in this rundown removes or replaces any existing scene. Items marked **[NEW]** are additions only.

## The story

### Prologue — The Island
The protagonist is on a plane with their sports team when it crashes on an island. As the only survivor, the protagonist runs through burning wreckage and finds a VHS tape. The memories the tape triggers begin the journey through the seven days.

### Day One — Hollowmere Galleria (Abandoned Mall)
The protagonist enters a glamorous abandoned mall called Hollowmere Galleria and meets **Keeper Thatch**, a stitched mascot who says, **"the system does not kill users. it reassigns them."** The mall floods. The protagonist descends into the foundations beneath the mall and is chased by a silent drowned figure. The protagonist is captured by a claw.

### Day Two — The Nostalgia Warehouse
The day opens in a real burning area and then cools into an endless nostalgic warehouse. The protagonist pushes a shopping trolley representing the **Morrow Moppet** and meets **The Fog**, who herds the protagonist through collapsing aisles. The day ends in the Fire Room.

### Day Three — Render Distance
The world is unfinished: an upside-down waterfall, floating islands and upside-down clouds. The **Velocity Wraith** warns, "The world hasn't finished loading," and **Fennix** argues with it. The protagonist encounters **the Cleaverback**, the mirror creature, and later follows the character in first person. A debugging chamber shows alternate outcomes, and the protagonist falls through a door marked **404 — WORLD NOT FOUND**. **[NEW]** Before the chase, the protagonist feels oddly at home, as if waking up in their own house after a dream. A siren repeats, each time more distorted. News reports about the accident play, along with death certificates and missing-person flyers. A small lost child asks for help finding safety, and the protagonist follows. Late in the day, **the Burned Accomplice** takes over the protagonist: no control, the protagonist is led into a burning pentagram surrounded by forgotten souls. An older witch in white gives a ritual: place four stones at north, south, east and west. If the fire goes out, the attempt fails.

### Day Four — 404 Forest / 99 Nights
A forest where the night keeps resetting ("NIGHT 01 / 99", then "NIGHT 02 / 99"). The **Broadcast Giant** calls out false rescue messages. The gentle **Pale Thread** guides the protagonist to a root tunnel with the warning **"THEY ARE NOT ALL HUNTERS. SOME REMEMBER WHAT HAPPENED."** The night ends at a door with cartoon animals, and the protagonist enters the next world. **[NEW]** The 99-night milestones all stay, and a single long night is added as an extra sequence in the same day.

### Day Five — Everybody Smiles Here
A painted cartoon street, where **Grin Cat** stretches to block the way and **Hound** turns its head without moving its body. The protagonist passes behind painted curtains, and the ink factory's projector tries to erase the protagonist's double. **Ink Man** erupts and chases the protagonist through a three-way pursuit. The protagonist reaches a film-editing machine, is told "You keep ruining the ending," and answers "Then stop writing it." The film strip tears and the world breaks open. **[NEW]** The chapter also gains a crawlspace section, **"Bodies in the Crawlspace,"** added before the studio: a claustrophobic space with the dead and a man who talks to them. **The Burned Accomplice** crawls like a spider and captures the protagonist, who wakes in a hatch room where the dead hang around and a television replays the prologue plane crash.

### Day Six — The Broadcast
The protagonist lands on a children's show set. **Miss Merrily** is controlling and asks "Which door takes us home?" Three doors each lead to a wrong answer. **Buttonbell** whispers a warning: "She doesn't control the show." The protagonist walks a lifeless neighbourhood where **Mr. Stillwell** says "Someone is watching you through your own eyes." At the Keepsake facility, animatronics say "You will be remembered forever." In the control room, a diagram explains the process, and the protagonist learns "Trapped? No. Preserved." **SPLICEMASTER** explains that the tape recruits a replacement central consciousness, and the protagonist smashes the console. The sets collapse into the final channel, a countdown, and a black chamber.

**[NEW]** The Day Six ritual sequence is added here, after the broadcast: the **south stone** sits in the basement of "Big Brother's" house. Placing it locks the protagonist in the room, and a radio gives instructions to say goodbye to friends, set oneself on fire, and perform a holy cleanse in the toilet, which Big Brother has prayed over. The protagonist is told the creature inside can only be freed through the pain of the burning, then passes out.

### Day Seven — The Sub-Bass Core
The protagonist wakes to birds and crows outside, and the way down leads to the core. The screen whites out and the protagonist opens their eyes in a **hospital bed**, having been in a coma for seven days. Life support was about to be turned off. **[NEW]** The protagonist leaves the hospital, hears the siren again, and the sound fades. The protagonist arrives at the first campsite beside the plane wreckage, and it starts over.

**Original ending kept:** the Core confrontation with SPLICEMASTER, the memory revelation, the final offer, the Splicemaster transformation, the collapsing-world chase, the camera being destroyed, the living room, and the on-screen sequence **INSTALLING NEW HOST… / MEMORY TRANSFER COMPLETE. / IDENTITY REASSIGNED. / INSTALLATION SUCCESSFUL. / PLAY AGAIN?** all remain. The hospital ending is an additional path.

## Open decisions

1. **Day Five and Day Six.** Currently planned as separate chapters. Your note said to combine them; the merge caused a load failure and is paused.

2. **Two endings.** The original living-room ending and the new hospital ending both exist. Which is canon, or does the hospital ending lead into a new cycle that ends in the living room?

3. **Ritual content.** Self-immolation is graphic. Do you want it shown directly, implied off-screen, or handled another way?

4. **Game-over on the stones.** Failing the ritual could be a game-over, or a retry of the puzzle.

5. **Names.** "Big Brother," "Homies," the witch, and the Burned Accomplice need final original designs, and should not resemble any existing character.
